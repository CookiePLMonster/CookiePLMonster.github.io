A retro-styled 2.5D racer inspired by OutRun, with a short single-player campaign and multiplayer challenges on procedurally generated courses.
A simple game, but it utilizes procedural generation very well by using phrases instead of numerical seeds, so it's really easy to get others to play against you online
on "your" courses. It's also priced extremely well.
