An impressive rally game made by a solo dev on a custom engine. It started as a mobile game before getting ported to consoles and PC, so the handling is noticeably simplistic,
but the amount of content is impressive (albeit unlicensed), and the visuals are reminiscent of the late 2000s games. I'm yet to try the full game, but I liked what the demo showed.
On top of that, **Rush Rally 4** is in active development.
