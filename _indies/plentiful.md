An Early Access God-game sandbox where the player is tasked with transforming the terrain to provide the best living conditions for a tribe they take care of.
The simulation is grounded in physics, with water flowing freely and resources spreading.
Plentiful is a solo dev indie made by Obbe Vermeij, the technical director of the 3D era GTAs and GTA IV!
