A cel-shaded arcade racer full of 90s references and a load of game modes, stunting, customization, and more. While I personally don't vibe with the fact that the game is trying to do
"everything" and includes every random 90s reference you can think of, the driving itself is quite nice, everything in the game is done competently, and it has **a lot** of content,
so those who like this style will have fun.
