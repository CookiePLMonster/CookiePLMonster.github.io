An ambitious open-world, story-driven racer set in Japan, with a heavy focus on drifting. The game has a mix of licensed and unlicensed vehicles and extensive visual
and mechanical customization. I haven't finished the story yet, but I enjoyed cruising and listening to the game's soundtrack a lot.
It also looks beautiful if your PC can handle it.
