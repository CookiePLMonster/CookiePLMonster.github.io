A 1v1 highway racer with cel-shaded graphics and semi-realistic handling. Vibes borrow heavily from the Tokyo Xtreme Racer games, but with regular closed-circuit races with traffic.
It can be finished in a single session, but it's a free remaster of the original game, released as a teaser for Highway Warriors 2.
