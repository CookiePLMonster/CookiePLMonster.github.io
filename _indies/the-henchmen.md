A solo dev take on a game combining the gameplay of top-down GTAs and Hotline Miami. The entire game can be played solo or in split-screen co-op,
and that's exactly how I 100%'d the game myself. I think the game is more than solid: the city is reasonably big, it offers some side activities,
and the story, although basic, is split into two parts, not unlike Mafia 2.
