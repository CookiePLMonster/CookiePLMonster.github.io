A mix of a motorsport simulation game with slot cars, where the player only controls the throttle and braking, and cars turn on their own.
Despite simplistic controls, the game has pretty elaborate physics, tire wear and fuel, and a very in-depth career mode where the players lead their drivers
throughout their entire careers. I didn't think much of the demo initially, but it surprised me with how complex and big in scope the career promises to be.
