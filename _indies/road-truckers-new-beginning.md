A truck simulator with elements of RPG and survival. From the public preview, it seems promising; I like that they are aiming to be
something more than a driving simulator and they incorporate more in-depth systems.
A demo is available on Steam: I tried a public playtest recently and I thought the setting and the sense of "building something"
makes it stand out from the other truck sims.
