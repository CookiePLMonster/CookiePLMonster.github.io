An obsessively accurate tribute to the PS2-era Need for Speed games that looks and feels nearly like an alternate universe NFS Underground 2.
The demo offers a short sample of what the game is going to be like, with race and drift events, time trials, and car customization.
It handles well and is much more polished than you'd expect of a two-man indie project that is a homage to NFS.
