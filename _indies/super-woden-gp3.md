The newest entry in the Super Woden series that completely ditches the isometric/overhead camera in favour of the traditional chase camera,
but otherwise keeps the trademark Super Woden look. With the way the game is shaping up so far, it might be the closest we've ever gotten
to an "indie Gran Turismo" so far.
