A set of new patches for the Tokyo Xtreme Racer is now out:
* Remove Blur patches -- removes a 1-line offset from the blurring effect, preserving the Depth of Field. Improves image clarity, especially when upscaling.
  Already shipped with the latest nightly PCSX2.
* Raindrops In All Camera Modes -- makes on-screen rain droplets display in all camera modes.

Patches are available for all regional releases of:
* Tokyo Xtreme Racer 3
* Tokyo Xtreme Racer: Drift
* Kaido Racer
* Tokyo Xtreme Racer: Drift 2
* Racing Battle: C1 Grand Prix

In PCSX2, Remove Blur patches are also available for Fu-un Bakumatsu-den, Fu-un Shinsengumi and Ninkyouden: Toseinin Ichidaiki -- I'll add them to the blog at a later point.
