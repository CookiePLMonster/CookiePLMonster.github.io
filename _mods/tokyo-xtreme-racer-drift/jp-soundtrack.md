A patch fixing soundtrack assignment issues in TXR Drift, making the music match the original release of Kaidō Battle:
* Working unique music for the **Night Conquest Menu** and **Night Parking Lot**.
* Correct selection of **Night Race** and **Night Replay** music, matching KB.
* Fixed boss races using wrong music for the intros, and using a generic **Race Night** music during the battle.
* Fixed the incorrect music titles in the Music Player, and re-added "Deadly" and "Going Home" to the selection.
